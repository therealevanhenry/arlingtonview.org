Membership in the Arlington View Civic Association is open to homeowners, renters, and non-profit organizations residing within the defined boundaries of the Arlington View community. Membership terms are from September to August, in accordance with Article III, Section I of the association bylaws.

## How to join

There is no online form. A resident who wishes to join can contact the president at [president@arlingtonview.org](mailto:president@arlingtonview.org).
